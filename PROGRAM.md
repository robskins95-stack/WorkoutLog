Both routines additionally include 4–5 x 12 bodyweight squats and 2 x 12 dead bugs (12 total alternating repetitions per set). Squats can be done first and dead bugs early or at the end as preferred. Warm up with light sets as needed. All weights are per dumbbell unless explicitly indicated otherwise.

## Progression and substitutions

- Read this plan and the latest actual entries in sets.csv at the beginning of each workout. Adapt targets based on actual clean reps and effort. Do not assume the weights above remain current forever.
- Rest around 90–120 seconds between compound sets, longer if needed; 60–90 seconds for isolation sets.
- Prioritize controlled full-range repetitions and about 1–3 reps in reserve. Stop when technique deteriorates or discomfort occurs. Increase resistance only when all sets reach their target comfortably with good form; use the smallest available increment.
- Pullovers, reverse flyes and lying triceps extensions are new; the weights are trial targets, not established working weights. Use a lighter weight if needed. Pullovers are not a full substitute for rows; if uncomfortable or poorly tolerated, substitute chest-supported rows. For pullovers, avoid excessive lower-back arching and use a comfortable shoulder range.
- Farmer carry progression from 40 to 45 lb per hand is optional if the back and grip feel good; otherwise use the established 40 lb.
- Keep squats unweighted for now because of prior possible back discomfort with goblet squats. Avoid adding loaded hinges or squats until tolerated and discussed. Do not push through back pain.
- The exercise catalog does not yet contain the three new movements; add appropriate catalog entries when first logging confirmed sets. Do not count a planned exercise as completed.
- Log only actual completed sets, reps, weight and user-reported effort/notes. Follow the existing CSV conventions in README.md.
